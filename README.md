# Soviet School 42V Converter
Convert Australian 240VAC power to Soviet 42VAC for computers & calculators.<br>

The Soviet had a low-voltage (42V) power supply available that used a special plug & socket (розетка 42В).<br>

![Soviet LV socket](/Images/old-lighting_ru_rozetka-na-42-v.jpg)

This was used by calculators such as the «Электроника МКШ-2» and computers such as the «Электроника МС0511».<br>

This appears similar to the Clipsal 402/32 (socket) and 492/32 (plug) available in Australia for low voltage requirements.<br>

![Clipsal plug](/Images/492-32TR.png)

![Clipsal socket](/Images/402-32.png)

My idea was to create a simple 240VAC-to-42VAC step-down power supply with matching socket.<br>

My initial design will be using the Triad Magentics FP40-600 transformer.<br>

I'm not sure if the Soviet plug will fit the Australian socket, but the plug can always be replaced.<br>
